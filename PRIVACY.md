# Privacy Policy - Trello Copy List as Text

This extension collects, stores, and transmits no personal data.

- It runs only on `trello.com` board pages.
- When you click a list's copy button, it reads that list's card titles from
  the page and fetches those cards' descriptions from Trello (`trello.com`)
  so they can be written to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- It uses no analytics, no tracking, no third-party servers, and no cookies
  of its own.
